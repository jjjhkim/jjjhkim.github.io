About

A little about my work

I am a Principal Researcher at the Electronics and Telecommunications Research Institute (ETRI) and an Assistant Professor at the University of Science and Technology (UST). My work has focused on wireless access technologies, standardization, and communication systems for challenging mobile environments.

I received my Ph.D. and M.S. degrees in Electrical Engineering from KAIST and my B.E. degree in Electronic Engineering from Tsinghua University.

5G / 6G Wireless Access
